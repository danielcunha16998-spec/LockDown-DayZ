Know before you go

FIELD GUIDES

Quick starting points for new survivors. Check Discord for the latest event details and server-specific updates.

Helicopter Crash Sites

Crash sites can contain valuable loot—or very little. Check every site you discover, stay alert while looting and never assume the area is safe.

Keys & Keycards

Keep track of the keys and keycards you find. They can give you access to restricted areas. Check the latest LOCKDOWN guide in Discord for current locations and requirements.

Base Building & Raiding

Choose your base location carefully, reinforce your defences and assume that another team may eventually find it. Raids are enabled 24/7.

Events & High-Value Loot

Events attract attention. Prepare your loadout, plan an escape route and remember that other survivors may be waiting for the same reward.

Custom Toxic Zone

Do not enter unprepared. Bring the required protection and check current server guidance before attempting a run.

Trading & Gold Rewards

PLAY EVENTS • EARN GOLD • GET REWARDED. Check Discord for current event rules, reward details and trader information.

Guides are starter information. Exact item requirements, loot tables and event timings may change with server updates.
